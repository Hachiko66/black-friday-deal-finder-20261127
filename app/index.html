    How we check a deal

    Every price on this page was read on 27 November 2026, either on the store’s own product page or in a deals roundup that names the store. Each card says where its price was read and when. The discount is always computed from the sale price and the usual price shown — never typed in by hand.

    Each deal also carries an honest signal. NEW LOW means the cheapest price we have ever tracked for it. STRONG DEAL means a deep, genuine cut. ROUTINE SALE means the discount is real but ordinary — the same price shows up often. Where a headline discount flatters the saving, the card says so.
